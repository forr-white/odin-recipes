Tres Leches Banana Pudding

Developed by Forrest White & Cook Anything Kitchen

desserts • Fusion

Ingredients

For the Tres Leches Custard Base:

  • 1/3 cup cornstarch
  • 1/4 tsp salt
  • 5 large egg yolks
  • 1 can (14 oz) sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 can (12 oz) evaporated milk
  • 1 cup whole milk
  • 2 tbsp unsalted butter
  • 1 tbsp pure vanilla extract (or vanilla bean paste)

For the Whipped Cream & Layers:

  • 2 cups heavy whipping cream, very cold
  • 2 tbsp powdered sugar
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • 5 to 6 ripe bananas, sliced
  • 1 box (11 oz) Nilla Wafers (or homemade vanilla wafers)

    • Directions

    1. Whisk the Base: In a medium bowl, whisk the cornstarch, salt, and egg yolks together until smooth and pale. In a heavy-bottomed medium saucepan (off the heat), whisk together the sweetened condensed milk, evaporated milk, and whole milk.
    2. Temper and Cook the Custard: Ladle about 1/2 cup of the cold milk mixture into the egg yolks, whisking vigorously to temper the eggs so they do not scramble. Pour the egg mixture back into the saucepan with the rest of the milk. Place over medium heat, whisking constantly, until the mixture begins to bubble and thicken into a rich pudding (about 8-10 minutes). Do not walk away from the stove, as cornstarch custards can scorch quickly on the bottom.
    3. Flavor and Chill: Remove the saucepan from the heat immediately. Stir in the butter and 1 tablespoon of vanilla extract until smooth. Pour the hot custard into a large bowl. Press a piece of plastic wrap directly onto the surface of the pudding to prevent a skin from forming. Refrigerate for at least 3 hours, or until completely cold.
    4. Make the Whipped Cream: Once the custard is cold, pour the heavy cream, powdered sugar, and 1 teaspoon of vanilla into a large bowl. Using a hand mixer, whip the cream until stiff peaks form.
    5. Lighten the Custard: Take about one-third of the freshly whipped cream and gently fold it into the cold tres leches custard using a rubber spatula until no white streaks remain. Reserve the rest of the whipped cream for the top layer.
    6. Assemble and Set: In a 9x13 dish or a large trifle bowl, create a tight layer of Nilla Wafers, followed by an even layer of sliced bananas. Spread half of the lightened custard over the bananas. Repeat with another layer of wafers, bananas, and the remaining custard. Top the entire dish with the reserved whipped cream. Refrigerate for at least 4 hours (or overnight) before serving so the wafers soften into cake-like layers.
