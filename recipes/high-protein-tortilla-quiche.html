High Protein Tortilla Quiche

Developed by Forrest White & Cook Anything Kitchen

breakfast • Breakfast

Ingredients

  • 1 Carb Balance Tortilla
  • 4 eggs
  • 75g 2% Cottage Cheese
  • 28g sharp cheddar cheese
  • 50g feta cheese
  • 10g grated Parmesan cheese
  • 30g fresh spinach
  • 100g tomato

Directions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350ºF
  2. Spray a pie dish or oven-safe pan with cooking spray, press your tortilla into the bottom, then add the remaining ingredients and mix everything together.
  3. Bake for 20-25 minutes, or until the center is set and the top is lightly golden.
  4. Let cool for a few minutes, slice into 4 even pieces, and enjoy.

Nutritional Information

Serving Size: 4 Servings

Calories: 192

Fat: 13 g

Carbohydrates: 10 g

Protein: 14 g
