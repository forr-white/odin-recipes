Low Fat Pinto Beans

Developed by Forrest White & Cook Anything Kitchen

main-course • Mexican

Ingredients

  • 1 lb dry pinto beans, sorted and rinsed
  • 1 medium yellow onion, finely diced
  • 5 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jalapeño or serrano pepper, seeded and diced (optional, for gentle heat)
  • 6 cups low-sodium vegetable or chicken broth (or water)
  • 1 tsp smoked paprika (provides the classic smoky depth usually supplied by bacon)
  • 1 tsp ground cumin
  • 1 tsp dried Mexican oregano (crushed between your palms)
  • 2 dried bay leaves
  • 1 tsp kosher salt (add near the end)
  • ½ tsp freshly cracked black pepper
  • Fresh cilantro and lime wedges, for serving

Directions

  1. Sauté Aromatics Without Excess Oil, Heat a large Dutch oven or stockpot over medium heat. Add the diced onion and jalapeño along with 2 to 3 tablespoons of broth or water. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes until soft and translucent, splashing in an extra splash of liquid if the pan dries out. Stir in the minced garlic and cook for 60 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Bloom Spices & Combine, Add the cumin, smoked paprika, and crushed Mexican oregano directly into the vegetables. Stir continuously for 30 seconds to wake up the spices, then pour in the rinsed pinto beans, bay leaves, and the remaining 6 cups of broth.
  3. Simmer Until Tender, Bring the liquid to a rolling boil over medium-high heat. Once boiling, reduce heat to low, cover with a tight-fitting lid, and let simmer gently for 1.5 to 2 hours (or until beans are tender all the way through). Stir occasionally and add water if the level dips below the beans.
  4. Season and Thicken Naturally, Once the beans are soft, remove the bay leaves and stir in the salt and black pepper (adding salt too early can toughen bean skins). Take a potato masher or the back of a large spoon and smash roughly ½ cup of the beans against the side of the pot. Stir well and simmer uncovered for another 8–10 minutes; the released starches will turn the cooking broth creamy and thick.
  5. Finish & Serve, Ladle into bowls with the cooking liquid. Top with chopped fresh cilantro and a generous squeeze of fresh lime juice to brighten the earthy flavors.

Notes

  • Pressure Cooker (Instant Pot) Variation
  • Follow Steps 1 & 2 using the Sauté function.
  • Secure the lid, seal the vent, and cook on High Pressure for 45 minutes (unsoaked) or 25 minutes (if soaked overnight).
  • Allow a natural pressure release for 15 minutes before venting the remaining steam, then proceed to Step 4 to season and mash for thickness.

Nutritional Information

Serving Size: 6 servings

Calories: 225

Fat: 1 g

Carbohydrates: 42 g

Protein: 12 g
